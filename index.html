First, know this
Toothache or swelling that won't go away, or a knocked-out tooth? That's urgent — call one of the clinics below and ask for the next available emergency or same-day appointment. If your face is swelling badly, you're running a fever, or you're having trouble breathing or swallowing, go to an emergency room — this is not something to wait out.

For everything else — a cleaning, a check-up, a cavity that isn't hurting yet — that's preventive care, and it's worth booking ahead rather than waiting for it to become an emergency.
