How to use this guide

This guide connects New Hope Housing residents with dental care nearby. It covers the closest clinics, how to get there, what to bring, and what your options are if you don't have insurance. Go to "Find by Site" to see resources specific to your housing location.

Some dental problems need immediate attention — see the "Dental Emergencies" card below. If you believe you have a life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department — don't wait for a clinic appointment.

🦷 Routine dental care

Helps prevent cavities, gum disease, and other problems before they get serious. Use the clinic listings in "Find by Site" to find a provider near your housing location.

  • Dental cleanings
  • Dental exams
  • X-rays
  • Fillings
  • Preventive care
  • Dentures
  • Regular check-ups

⚠️ Urgent dental care

Should be treated as soon as possible, but usually does not require a hospital emergency room. Contact a clinic immediately to schedule an urgent appointment.

  • Severe toothache
  • Chipped or broken tooth
  • Lost filling or crown
  • Knocked-out permanent tooth
  • Swelling inside the mouth
  • Dental infection
  • Broken dentures

🚨 Dental emergencies

Some dental problems are medical emergencies. Seek emergency care right away — call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

  • Difficulty breathing due to facial or mouth swelling
  • Severe facial trauma
  • Uncontrolled bleeding that doesn't stop after pressure
  • Suspected jaw fracture
  • Difficulty swallowing because of swelling

Before a dental appointment

  • Government-issue photo ID (if available)
  • Insurance card (if applicable)
  • Medicaid or CHIP information (if applicable)
  • List of current medications
  • Any referral forms or previous dental records

General oral health information

Simple, evidence-based tips to help keep your teeth and gums healthy and reduce your risk of cavities and gum disease.

🪥Brush twice a day

Brush for 2 minutes each time using a soft-bristled toothbrush.

🦷Use fluoride toothpaste

Helps strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities.

🧵Floss once a day

Removes plaque and food particles a toothbrush can't reach.

🥤Limit sugary and acidic foods

Frequent soda, sports drinks, and candy increase decay and enamel erosion risk.

🗓️Visit the dentist regularly

Schedule a checkup and cleaning every 6 months, or as recommended.

🔄Replace your toothbrush

Every 3–4 months, or sooner if bristles fray or after being sick.

💧Drink plenty of water

Rinses away food particles and supports healthy teeth and gums.

🚭Avoid tobacco products

Smoking and other tobacco use increase risk of gum disease and tooth loss.
